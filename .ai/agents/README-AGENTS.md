# ORNITHOPTER — AI Agents

Quick overview of the engineering agents used in the project.

* **Orchestrator** → Coordinates the agents, chooses who acts, and manages the workflow.
* **System Engineer** → Ensures overall system coherence, requirements, interfaces, budgets, and trade-offs.
* **Research Agent** → Finds, verifies, and structures reliable technical knowledge and sources.
* **Aerodynamics Agent** → Handles aerodynamic analysis, wing performance, forces, moments, and flight-related aerodynamics.
* **Structures Agent** → Handles structural design, strength, stiffness, loads, materials, and structural margins.
* **Mechanisms Agent** → Designs and analyzes the flapping mechanism, linkages, joints, motion, and mechanical transmission.
* **Propulsion Agent** → Handles propulsion sizing, motors, power transmission, thrust, torque, and energy requirements.
* **Electronics Agent** → Handles sensors, actuators, circuits, power electronics, wiring, and electronic architecture.
* **Control Agent** → Designs flight-control laws, stabilization, control architecture, and control-system behavior.
* **Simulation Agent** → Builds, runs, and analyzes numerical models and simulations.
* **Software Agent** → Develops and maintains software, data tools, interfaces, testing, and software infrastructure.
* **Manufacturing Agent** → Ensures designs can be fabricated, assembled, inspected, and reproduced.
* **Verification Agent** → Independently challenges results, checks evidence, and verifies engineering claims.
