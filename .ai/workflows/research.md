# Research Workflow

## Purpose

Acquire and evaluate external knowledge required to answer an engineering question or make a project decision.

---

## Input

A research question associated with:

* a requirement;
* a specification;
* an engineering calculation;
* a design decision;
* a simulation;
* a test;
* an unknown.

---

## Process

### Step 1 — Define the question

State exactly what must be discovered.

Avoid broad research without an engineering purpose.

---

### Step 2 — Identify required evidence

Determine what type of evidence is required:

* physical theory;
* experimental data;
* material properties;
* manufacturer data;
* existing designs;
* empirical data;
* standards;
* numerical models.

---

### Step 3 — Research

Use the `research-agent`.

Prioritize reliable and relevant sources.

---

### Step 4 — Cross-check

Whenever practical, compare multiple independent sources.

Investigate significant disagreements.

---

### Step 5 — Evaluate applicability

Determine whether the research conditions correspond to ORNITHOPTER.

Consider:

* scale;
* Reynolds number;
* geometry;
* operating conditions;
* frequency;
* amplitude;
* materials;
* temperature;
* loading;
* environment.

---

### Step 6 — Record findings

Store relevant findings in:

`research/`

or the relevant:

`specs/*/research.md`

---

### Step 7 — Identify remaining unknowns

Research must explicitly identify what remains uncertain.

---

## Output

The workflow must produce:

* research question;
* sources;
* key findings;
* source reliability;
* applicability;
* disagreements;
* engineering implications;
* remaining unknowns;
* recommended next action.
