# Calculate Workflow

## Purpose

Perform an engineering calculation using explicit assumptions, traceable inputs, appropriate models, and verifiable results.

---

## Input

A defined engineering problem with:

* known parameters;
* requirements;
* constraints;
* applicable physical models;
* required output.

---

## Process

### Step 1 — Define the objective

State exactly what must be calculated.

---

### Step 2 — Collect inputs

For every input identify:

* value;
* unit;
* source;
* uncertainty when relevant.

---

### Step 3 — Identify assumptions

Explicitly state all significant assumptions.

---

### Step 4 — Select the model

Determine the appropriate:

* physical law;
* equation;
* mathematical model;
* empirical correlation;
* numerical model.

Explain why the model is appropriate.

---

### Step 5 — Check dimensions

Verify dimensional consistency.

---

### Step 6 — Perform the calculation

Show sufficient intermediate steps to make the calculation auditable.

---

### Step 7 — Perform sanity checks

Check whether the result is physically plausible.

Use:

* order-of-magnitude checks;
* limiting cases;
* comparisons with known values;
* independent calculations when practical.

---

### Step 8 — Sensitivity

When relevant, determine which inputs have the largest influence on the result.

---

### Step 9 — Document

Store important calculations in:

`engineering/calculations/`

or the relevant specification directory.

---

## Output

The result must contain:

* objective;
* inputs;
* sources;
* assumptions;
* model;
* equations;
* calculation;
* result;
* units;
* sanity check;
* uncertainty;
* limitations;
* next action.
