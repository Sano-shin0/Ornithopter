# Design Workflow

## Purpose

Transform validated requirements, research, engineering analysis, and constraints into a coherent design.

---

## Input

The design process may use:

* requirements;
* research;
* calculations;
* models;
* simulations;
* available components;
* manufacturing constraints;
* safety constraints.

---

## Process

### Step 1 — Define the design problem

Identify the requirements and constraints that the design must satisfy.

---

### Step 2 — Generate alternatives

When meaningful, create multiple candidate solutions.

Do not assume the first plausible solution is optimal.

---

### Step 3 — Define evaluation criteria

Possible criteria include:

* mass;
* efficiency;
* power;
* performance;
* reliability;
* manufacturability;
* cost;
* complexity;
* safety;
* maintainability.

---

### Step 4 — Compare alternatives

Use engineering analysis and trade studies.

Avoid subjective selection when quantitative comparison is possible.

---

### Step 5 — Select a baseline design

Choose the solution that best satisfies the project's requirements and constraints.

---

### Step 6 — Check interfaces

Evaluate interactions with:

* structure;
* aerodynamics;
* propulsion;
* electronics;
* control;
* manufacturing;
* power system.

---

### Step 7 — Document the decision

Create or update the appropriate engineering decision record.

---

### Step 8 — Define verification

Determine how the design will later be verified.

---

## Output

The design record should contain:

* design objective;
* requirements;
* alternatives;
* evaluation criteria;
* analysis;
* selected design;
* justification;
* interfaces;
* risks;
* assumptions;
* verification method;
* remaining uncertainties.
