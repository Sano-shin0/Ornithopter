# 3. Critical Parameters

The following parameters are expected to dominate feasibility:

* total mass,
* wing area,
* flapping frequency,
* stroke amplitude,
* wing kinematics,
* aerodynamic efficiency,
* actuator power,
* mechanism efficiency,
* battery specific energy,
* and flight speed.

---

# 4. Major Risks

The principal technical risks currently identified are:

1. Insufficient aerodynamic force.
2. Excessive mechanical power requirement.
3. Excessive total mass.
4. Insufficient actuator torque.
5. Mechanical fatigue.
6. Excessive vibration.
7. Insufficient energy density.
8. Flight instability.
9. Insufficient control authority.
10. Excessive complexity.

---

# 5. Decision Criteria

The project shall proceed to detailed design if the feasibility analysis demonstrates that:

* aerodynamic force generation is plausible,
* required mechanical power is achievable,
* the actuator can satisfy the mechanical requirements,
* the energy system can support the mission,
* the estimated mass remains self-consistent,
* and the control problem is experimentally tractable.

---

# 6. Decision

**Current decision:** PENDING ANALYSIS

No final hardware architecture shall be frozen before completion of the preliminary feasibility study.

---

# 7. Next Step

The next step is to create the first quantitative model of ORNITHOPTER using:

* preliminary body dimensions,
* preliminary wing dimensions,
* estimated mass range,
* air properties,
* candidate flapping frequency range,
* and candidate stroke amplitude range.

The model shall produce first-order estimates of:

* lift,
* thrust,
* power,
* wing loading,
* and energy consumption.
